# Distillate: Promptotyping paper, version 0.9

The paper that consolidates Promptotyping as a method. It supplies the vault with the definition of the method, its four recurrent forms of work, the document types of the project knowledge base, the four forms of checking with their differing authority, the comparative reading of the documented project cases, and the declared evidential limits of the whole claim.

## Core statements

- Scholarly work with digital research data is mediated by software, and a tool operationalises only those distinctions that have been encoded in structures its representational and operational model can process. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0002]] ^s001
- Large language models can reduce the effort of mapping research data to the models existing software expects and of implementing more closely adapted ways of working with those data; where the relevant structure is not yet explicit, the step that recognises or interprets it is probabilistic. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0003]] ^s002
- Mapping research data into an existing tool confines subsequent work to the distinctions, operations, and forms of interaction that the tool provides. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0004]] ^s003
- Technical verification assesses whether an output conforms to formalised requirements, while scholarly validation assesses whether the underlying representation is warranted by the source material and adequate for its intended research purpose, so an implementation may be technically correct and still fail scholarly validation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0005]] ^s004
- The name Promptotyping combines prompt and prototyping and retains the established function of a prototype as a provisional implementation through which requirements and design possibilities can be examined and refined. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0006]] ^s005
- Digital research artefacts are project-specific operational forms through which researchers work with structured research data for a defined scholarly purpose, and what counts as research data depends on the disciplinary and project context in which material is selected and interpreted. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0007]] ^s006
- Formal modelling and FAIR preparation support machine-actionability, interoperability, and reuse, and they do not determine how researchers should work with the data in a particular project, because the same data may support different research questions and therefore require different operational forms. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0008]] ^s007
- Digital tool criticism has shown that software participates in scholarly knowledge production rather than serving as a neutral means of access, so generic tools may require researchers to adapt their inquiry to assumptions developed for other purposes. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0009]] ^s008
- Project-specific digital research artefacts remain selective and allow those selections to be made and examined in relation to the project's own data and purpose. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0009]] ^s009
- Scholar-Centred Design was developed as a method for translating scholarly perspectives into project-specific requirements, and two contrasting use cases on a shared model of historical accounting transactions showed that an interoperable representation supports different forms of inquiry without determining which distinctions should be foregrounded or which operations and interface structures each investigation requires. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0010]] ^s010
- Translating scholarly requirements into software has commonly involved collaboration with Research Software Engineers, which remains essential for artefacts that require dependable long-term operation, institutional integration, security, or supported use by third parties, and the necessary expertise and resources are not equally available to individual researchers and small projects. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0011]] ^s011
- Not all relevant requirements can be determined in advance, and provisional artefacts can reveal problems in the specification, show what the data can and cannot support, and thereby make implementation part of determining how researchers wish to work with their data. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0012]] ^s012
- The effective and responsible use of language models for extended software development depends on the information available to the system, the organisation of the work performed by agents, and the technical environment through which they interact with a project, beyond the choice of model and the formulation of a prompt. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0013]] ^s013
- Context Engineering is the systematic selection, organisation, maintenance, and provision of the information a language-model-based system requires for its work, extending Prompt Engineering from individual prompts to the wider informational environment in which they are interpreted, and it does not consist in placing all available material into a context window. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0014]] ^s014
- An AI agent is a language-model-based system that pursues a goal through a sequence of tool-supported actions and adapts to intermediate results, and Agentic Engineering is the systematic organisation of this extended work, covering task decomposition, tool use, the point at which human intervention is required, and how the work is inspected and continued. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0015]] ^s015
- An AI harness is the technical environment through which an agent receives context, accesses project resources, uses tools, and obtains feedback, so agentic capability arises from the combined model, harness, and environment rather than from the model alone. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0016]] ^s016
- Promptotyping developed through language-model-supported experiments in research and teaching since 2023, and its specification, document templates, and revision history are maintained in a public repository. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0019]] ^s017
- Promptotyping is an iterative, knowledge-driven method for developing project-specific digital research artefacts from structured research data and maintained project knowledge through Context Engineering and Agentic Engineering, and its organising structure is an evolving and versioned project knowledge base into which findings from implementation and examination are written back. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0020]] ^s018
- The method is knowledge-driven because implementation proceeds from a maintained account of how the data are understood and are to be operationalised rather than from the research data alone, and the knowledge base takes a semi-formal documentary form that supports no formal inference. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0021]] ^s019
- The project knowledge base consists of interrelated documents that are bounded representations distilled from fuller research material, and its three document types are organisational heuristics rather than a prescribed file structure. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0022]] ^s020
- Declarative knowledge documents state what the project currently takes to be the case and what the artefact is required to realise, covering the structure, semantics, provenance, gaps, and uncertainties of the data, the connection between scholarly practices and expected system behaviour, and how modelled distinctions should be represented in the artefact. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0023]] ^s021
- Process knowledge documents preserve how the project's understanding developed and why decisions were taken, recording attempted approaches, failures, and unresolved questions, and together with version control they make changes and their rationale traceable. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0024]] ^s022
- Agent instruction documents translate maintained project knowledge into operational guidance for agentic work, and keeping these comparatively volatile instructions separate from more stable accounts of the data and scholarly purpose prevents temporary implementation directives from silently altering the project's maintained understanding. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0025]] ^s023
- Projects whose agents act across several persistent resources or handle consequential claims, rights, and release decisions may maintain a versioned project governance document that records authority, permissions, evidential status, rights and release conditions, write-back targets, and escalation paths. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0026]] ^s024
- Derived project artefacts are regenerable outputs produced from a referenced project state through an identified process, and they remain distinct from maintained project knowledge because they record derived observations rather than interpretations or decisions that contributors have examined and adopted. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0027]] ^s025
- The persistent project knowledge base must be distinguished from an agent's task-specific working context, which holds the information and access required for a particular assignment and may draw on retrieval and direct access to project resources without replacing the maintained account of purpose, data semantics, and criteria of assessment. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0028]] ^s026
- Context Engineering organises the task-specific information and project access available to an agent while Agentic Engineering structures how the agent acts within those conditions, and the two are interdependent, so a carefully maintained knowledge base does not determine an adequate course of action and a well-organised workflow cannot compensate for an inadequate account of the data. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0029]] ^s027
- Promptotyping is not tied to a particular documentary environment or mechanism for assembling working context, and it treats selection as part of Context Engineering rather than using accumulation as a substitute for it. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0029]] ^s028
- Promptotyping is organised around four recurrent phases of work, Preparation, Exploration, Distillation, and Implementation, which are analytically distinct without forming a fixed or exclusively linear sequence, and findings arising from Implementation may return the work to any earlier phase, most frequently to Distillation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0031]] ^s029
- The Critical Expert specifies and revises project knowledge, verifies the artefact, and accepts the iteration for its stated purpose, thereby establishing an identifiable promptotype. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0033]] ^s030
- Preparation assembles the source basis and research context, brings the relevant research material into an accessible project environment, and does not require that material to be normalised, formally modelled, or placed directly in an agent's working context, requiring instead that it remain accessible for inspection and computational use with its provenance and known limitations documented. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0034]] ^s031
- Preparation gathers the scholar's current understanding and the project's provisional purpose as documented in existing working materials, and it is provisionally sufficient when the source basis and research context are digitally accessible and documented well enough to support systematic examination. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0035]] ^s032
- Exploration examines the prepared material to understand its structure and limitations and to determine what the project knowledge base must represent, rather than to formulate the maintained knowledge documents themselves. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0036]] ^s033
- The form of Exploration depends on the scale and structure of the material, so a small collection may be examined directly while large or heterogeneous collections require computational profiling, with the collection remaining in the project environment while the resulting profiles and selected examples enter the agent's working context. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0037]] ^s034
- The outputs of Exploration are provisional observations rather than maintained project knowledge, and they become part of the knowledge base only through interpretation and Distillation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0038]] ^s035
- Distillation translates the understanding produced through Preparation and Exploration into maintained knowledge documents from which implementation and verification can proceed, and it is the principal documentary operation of Context Engineering within Promptotyping. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0039]] ^s036
- Distillation is not reducible to summarisation or context compression, because a model's nominal context capacity does not imply that all supplied information will be used reliably and poorly selected material can make relevant information harder to use, so Distillation creates an inspectable and selective representation that preserves the distinctions, conditions, and uncertainties required for adequate implementation and verification. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0040]] ^s037
- The same material may need to be distilled differently when the project's purpose or intended artefact changes, which makes Distillation a form of pragmatic modelling for human contributors and agent instances. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0040]] ^s038
- The sufficiency of Distillation is practical rather than formal, in that a new contributor or agent instance given the relevant knowledge documents and access to project resources should be able to reconstruct the project's current logic and continue the assigned work without undocumented explanation, while successful implementation does not establish scholarly adequacy. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0041]] ^s039
- Implementation makes maintained project knowledge actionable through language-model-supported development, either through iterative chat-based interaction or, more effectively where the agent must work directly with project files and continue across several steps, through an AI harness, and it consists of bounded tasks whose consequences become available for examination. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0042]] ^s040
- Work proceeds through inspectable and versioned increments, each of which should establish a runnable state that can be compared with the maintained project knowledge before further assumptions become embedded in the implementation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0043]] ^s041
- Agentic implementation requires the project state to remain intelligible and testable, so refactoring and executable checks preserve the inspectability of the implementation, and findings that alter the project's maintained understanding initiate write-back to the relevant documents rather than remaining local corrections in the code. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0044]] ^s042
- Implementation is provisionally sufficient when the current artefact state is runnable, inspectable, and identifiable through versioning, and that state is not yet a promptotype but the basis from which purpose-specific verification and acceptance can proceed. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0045]] ^s043
- A correction becomes methodologically consequential when it is incorporated into maintained project knowledge, so write-back is the mechanism through which findings from Implementation become durable project knowledge rather than an additional phase. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0046]] ^s044
- A promptotype is the accepted iteration state in which the maintained project knowledge, the resulting digital research artefact, the referenced research-data state, and the documented grounds of acceptance form a coherent and identifiable state for a stated purpose, and a runnable or plausible artefact alone does not meet this threshold. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0046]] ^s045
- Acceptance remains the responsibility of the Critical Expert, understood as the person or group competent and accountable for judging whether the project knowledge adequately represents the research material and whether the artefact is suitable for its intended purpose, and an agent may contribute proposals and assessments without assuming responsibility for their adequacy. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0046]] ^s046
- The project cases are presented as methodologically guided reconstructions of documented project practice rather than as complete histories or comparative evaluations, each analysed through its initial project state, the interaction of the four forms of work, the findings arising from implementation, and their incorporation into revised project knowledge or purpose-specific acceptance. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0047]] ^s047
- The detailed cases cover an estate corpus transformed into structured textual research data, a staged transcription and encoding workflow at a research library, a project whose data model and capture practice were still developing, and a proposal-stage demonstrator, so that implementation is shown supporting data production and curation, prospective modelling, and project formation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0048]] ^s048
- In the estate-corpus workflow, selected information from existing encoded records was inserted into the prompt supplied with each manuscript image, and the classification of the object determined which task-specific transcription prompt the model received. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0050]] ^s049
- A controlled comparison in the estate-corpus workflow showed that richer metadata could introduce information from the prompt into a transcription even when that information was not visible in the image, and model-generated document classifications in the library workflow were treated as provisional hypotheses rather than authoritative descriptions. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0051]] ^s050
- The maintained knowledge directories of the two workflows record characteristics of the source material, processing requirements, annotation and checking conventions, methodological decisions, unresolved questions, and reasons for revising earlier approaches, so that Distillation preserved uncertainty and negative findings rather than converting incomplete evidence into apparently settled requirements. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0052]] ^s051
- Both repositories retain evidence of alternatives that were tested, restricted, replaced, or abandoned, so their public deployments are the latest inspectable states of documented development histories rather than the direct execution of stable initial specifications. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0053]] ^s052
- In scholarly research, adequacy depends not only on conformity to specified requirements but also on the interpretation of sources, the modelling decisions through which they are represented, and the purpose for which the resulting data are intended. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0054]] ^s053
- Deterministic verification tests conformity to formalised requirements through schemas, constraints, transformation tests, structural audits, and reproducible measurements, and its conclusions are limited to the properties encoded by the check. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0055]] ^s054
- Agentic review is a bounded, tool-supported investigation in which one or more language-model-based agents examine outputs, data states, implementations, or research artefacts against relevant sources, references, requirements, and criteria. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0056]] ^s055
- Critical Expert verification and adjudication is the accountable examination of particular outputs against their sources and the resolution of cases that deterministic or probabilistic procedures cannot determine, and it records who assumes responsibility for the resulting judgement. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0057]] ^s056
- Scholarly validation assesses whether the representations, requirements, evaluation criteria, and artefacts governing a workflow are warranted by the research material and adequate for their intended scholarly purpose. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0058]] ^s057
- An agentic reviewer can investigate a delimited part of a project by retrieving relevant materials, comparing several artefact layers, executing tools, and examining the grounds of apparent discrepancies, whereas an LLM-as-a-Judge typically evaluates a supplied output under a given reference or rubric and returns a score or judgement, so the latter can be one operation within agentic review without exhausting it. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0059]] ^s058
- The evidential value of agentic review depends on how the investigation is organised, and its findings remain probabilistic evidence rather than authorised verification, scholarly validation, or acceptance. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0060]] ^s059
- In the library workflow an earlier agent-screening process assigned approval labels although no responsible contributor had granted approval; the labels were abolished, agent findings were reclassified as provisional evidence pending operator adjudication, and the capacity to inspect an output was thereby separated from the authority to record it as verified. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0061]] ^s060
- Character error rate can be calculated reproducibly once reference text, extraction rules, normalisation, comparison scope, and calculation procedure have been specified, and it measures deviation from a selected reference rather than objective transcription correctness, so a deterministic metric can verify a formally specified comparison without validating the scholarly adequacy of its specification. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0062]] ^s061
- Acceptance is the accountable decision through which an identifiable iteration state becomes a promptotype for a stated purpose, and it may be bounded, so that an artefact can be accepted as an experimental processing pipeline or handover state without being accepted as a completed or publication-ready scholarly edition. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0063]] ^s062
- Agent-supported data production becomes methodologically accountable through a reconstructable relation among sources, maintained project knowledge, versioned implementation, differentiated evidence, and responsible scholarly judgement, and the methodological significance of such workflows lies also in rejected assumptions, detected validation gaps, withdrawn approval states, corrected readings, and documented limitations. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0064]] ^s063
- In a project whose data model and requirements were still developing, data capture and interface functionality were developed alongside the model, so that implementation became a means of examining and revising the emerging model in practice. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0065]] ^s064
- Rapidly developed interfaces allowed project partners to enter, display, filter, and compare information through a shared operational environment rather than discussing the model only through schemas and abstract descriptions, while leaving categories, relations, and capture requirements open to revision. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0067]] ^s065
- Interaction with those interfaces revealed where distinctions could be supported by the available material, where categories remained ambiguous, where additional information was required during capture, and where a technically possible visualisation would imply a degree of precision, completeness, or certainty that the data could not warrant. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0069]] ^s066
- Findings had to be interpreted before they could be written back, because some concerned the represented domain and required revision of the data model or capture practice while others concerned how an otherwise adequate distinction should be made accessible and could remain within the operational or visual design of the interface. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0070]] ^s067
- A digital research artefact can participate in the formation of the model, capture practice, and requirements from which it is developed, provided that the consequences disclosed through its use are interpreted, documented, and incorporated into maintained project knowledge. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0071]] ^s068
- Promptotyping can support project formation before a complete corpus, production workflow, or funded development process exists, by making a proposed editorial and technical arrangement functional enough to be examined and communicated within the preparation of a funding proposal. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0072]] ^s069
- In the proposal-stage case, Distillation translated the emerging editorial and organisational account into requirements and design decisions, and Implementation produced a bounded path from the initial material to an encoded representation and from that representation to a statically hosted Edition Interface. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0075]] ^s070
- The proposal-stage artefact allowed the planned project to be encountered as a functional arrangement rather than only as a textual description, and thereby exposed the practical consequences of the proposal before a production corpus and workflow had been established. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0076]] ^s071
- Purpose-specific acceptance of a demonstrator did not establish corpus-wide philological adequacy and did not imply that the provisional transformation, model, or interface could be transferred unchanged into later production, which shows that acceptance can stabilise a deliberately bounded project state without extending its claims beyond the materials examined. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0077]] ^s072
- The cases begin from different project states and foreground different functions of Implementation while each maintains a reconstructable relation among maintained project knowledge, a referenced research-data state, the resulting artefact, findings arising from its examination, and responsible judgement, so Promptotyping prescribes neither a single project chronology nor one artefact form. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0078]] ^s073
- Maintained project knowledge guides Implementation without determining a single adequate realisation, because natural-language descriptions retain ambiguity and different agent runs may realise the same requirement in materially different ways, so the Critical Expert must examine whether alternatives were excluded, conventions reproduced without justification, or absences concealed by an apparently coherent artefact. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0079]] ^s074
- Critical expertise may be held by one hybrid scholar-developer or distributed among contributors with complementary scholarly and technical competencies, and dividing agentic work among several agents does not transfer responsibility to them, requires their assignments and permissions to remain explicit and auditable, and may increase the work required to coordinate and audit their actions. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0080]] ^s075
- Acceptance is purpose-specific and does not imply that the project knowledge, research data, or artefact has become final, so a promptotype may be accepted as an exploratory interface, an internal production workflow, a proposal-stage demonstrator, a handover state, or a public research artefact, and new sources, corrected assumptions, changed purposes, or improved checks may initiate another iteration. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0081]] ^s076
- The accepted state must remain identifiable and reconstructable through a repository release, archived deposit, or another durable reference, without requiring a particular hosting platform or versioning scheme, and a renewed implementation using another model, harness, or project state constitutes a new iteration rather than a silent reproduction of the earlier promptotype. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0082]] ^s077
- A public interface or runnable artefact can establish that a particular implementation state existed and show which operations it made available, and it cannot by itself establish the history of that state, the decisions that informed it, the findings written back into project knowledge, or the authority through which verification or acceptance was assigned. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0084]] ^s078
- The comparison of the documented corpus is organised by four analytical dimensions, the project condition from which Implementation began, the primary methodological function Implementation performed, the operational form of the resulting artefact, and the level at which examination produced a consequential finding. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0085]] ^s079
- The four analytical dimensions may overlap and change between iterations, so that a data-capture interface may also support exploration and a production workflow may later become a handover package or an object of methodological examination. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0090]] ^s080
- Promptotyping can begin from an established research-data state, participate in the development of that state, or operationalise a prospective scholarly arrangement before a complete production corpus exists. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0091]] ^s081
- Production and curation as a methodological function makes operations for creating, transforming, checking, correcting, and exporting research data functional and inspectable, and its significance lies also in making the route from source material to differentiated output and checking states explicit. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0092]] ^s082
- Prospective modelling and exploration as a methodological function uses functional artefacts to examine an emerging model or the project-specific consequences of an existing representation, so that modelling and representation decisions can be assessed through the data to which they apply. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0093]] ^s083
- Project formation and bounded communication as a methodological function gives functional form to a proposed scholarly arrangement before production begins, with an accepted purpose of making the proposed project available for examination and communication. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0094]] ^s084
- The recurring operational forms of a promptotype are capture, which makes a data model actionable during structured data creation, transformation, which maps source or research data into another explicit representation, exploration, which supports investigation, comparison, filtering, and navigation, edition, which makes an editorial account of texts and their relations operational, verification, which supports the inspection of outputs, evidence, checking procedures, and differentiated states, and integrated scholarly workbenches, which combine several of these forms for a bounded research task. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0095]] ^s085
- The recurring structure of Promptotyping lies in the relation between maintained project knowledge and an artefact that makes selected scholarly operations available for examination, and no particular software form is constitutive of the method. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0096]] ^s086
- The documented promptotypes range from an estate corpus with existing encoded metadata through a staged production process with supplied references, a developing data model and capture practice, a bounded proposal sample, an existing correspondence corpus, heterogeneous user-supplied document images, and textual sources with an emerging encoding mapping, with bounded purposes from experimental production workflow to accepted demonstrator and research preview. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0098]] ^s087
- The documented cases produced findings at several interacting but non-interchangeable levels, covering the represented domain, source interpretation, research-data models, capture practices, transformations, interface representations, the organisation of agentic work, checking procedures, and the allocation of authority, so treating every observed problem as a defect in generated code would conceal the decisions through which the artefact was produced. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0099]] ^s088
- Attributing a finding to the layer that produced it prevents limitations of the model from being concealed through interface logic while avoiding unnecessary changes to the domain model, and although an implemented artefact makes the effects of several layers visible together, responsible interpretation must distinguish which layer produced the observed consequence. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0100]] ^s089
- A transformation may conform to its mapping and produce schema-valid output while representing the source inadequately, and an error in the implementation of an adequate mapping should be corrected in the transformation rather than in the mapping, so the implemented result allows conformity to be examined separately from the scholarly adequacy of the mapping. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0101]] ^s090
- Findings in interface and visual representation concern the claims implied by the way data are made visible and actionable, as when dates encoded with earliest and latest bounds could not warrant representation as precise points on a timeline and were represented as intervals to preserve the uncertainty already encoded in the structured data. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0102]] ^s091
- Problems in the organisation of agentic work, checking, and authority may require changes to working contexts, permissions, escalation procedures, agent instructions, verification states, or acceptance criteria rather than changes to the represented research domain. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0103]] ^s092
- Write-back must be directed to the level at which the underlying problem arises, its purpose is to incorporate the interpreted consequence of a finding into the maintained project state rather than to preserve every observation, and not every consequential finding requires revision, because implementation may instead establish the boundary of what an iteration can responsibly claim. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0104]] ^s093
- Project-level write-back changes the maintained knowledge, implementation conditions, or checking arrangements of a particular project, whereas method-level write-back incorporates a finding into more general rules, and generalisation depends on the available evidence, the recurrence of the problem across cases, and the consequences of adopting the proposed rule. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0105]] ^s094
- Agents may assemble evidence, compare materials, investigate discrepancies, execute checks, and record provisional assessments, and they may not independently assign an authorised verification status, scholarly validation, approval, or acceptance. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0106]] ^s095
- Qualitative degradation during extended implementation sessions prompted changes to session length, context refresh, and reliance on maintained project documents, which suggests at method level that implementation may reveal limitations in the conditions under which context is selected and agentic work is continued, and further comparison is required before this observation can become a general rule. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0107]] ^s096
- An observed failure becomes project-level or method-level learning only through interpretation, attribution, documentation, and incorporation into the maintained project state, and without these operations it remains a local implementation event. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0107]] ^s097
- Digital Humanities scholarship has long treated prototypes, interfaces, and visualisations as forms through which interpretative claims and research possibilities can be explored rather than merely presented, and the documented cases are consistent with this position. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0108]] ^s098
- The artefact does not produce scholarly knowledge autonomously, and its epistemic relevance arises through operationalisation, examination, responsible interpretation, attribution, and write-back rather than through generation or functionality alone. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0109]] ^s099
- Whereas a schema presents a model through formal notation, an interface can show how selected distinctions behave when data are entered, filtered, compared, and represented, which gives functional artefacts a complementary way of examining modelling decisions. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0110]] ^s100
- The documented practice does not establish that functional artefacts broaden participation, improve decisions, or produce findings that could not also have emerged through source criticism, specification review, data profiling, non-functional prototypes, or structured discussion, so artefact-mediated scholarly enquiry remains a conceptual proposition motivated by the cases rather than a demonstrated comparative advantage. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0111]] ^s101
- The variation across project conditions, artefact forms, and bounded purposes supports the workability and range of Promptotyping within the practice from which it was consolidated, and it does not establish causal effects attributable to the method, comparative superiority, independent transferability, or a complete typology of promptotypes. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0112]] ^s102
- Promptotyping was consolidated primarily through projects led by one hybrid scholar-developer, the cases do not constitute a controlled or representative sample, their documentation is subject to selection effects because inspectable implementation states were preserved more systematically than abandoned experiments, and observed improvements cannot be attributed cleanly to the method rather than to more capable systems, better tools, or increased experience. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0114]] ^s103
- The method can support the articulation, implementation, and revision of available understanding, and it cannot supply domain knowledge that practitioners do not possess or cannot recognise as relevant, so templates and maintained documents may make assumptions more explicit and revisable without compensating for missing competence. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0115]] ^s104
- Inadequate project knowledge can guide an agent towards an apparently coherent and operational implementation that remains inadequate in scholarly terms, so responsible application requires critical examination of the provenance, construction, scope, and representational limits of the data. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0115]] ^s105
- The maintained account of a project must be proportionate to the scope and consequences of the work being delegated, so a bounded transformation on a stable format may be guided by concise mappings and formal checks while work spanning interpretation, modelling, implementation, and verification requires a more explicit account of sources, assumptions, requirements, and limitations. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0116]] ^s106
- Promptotyping should not be treated as a substitute for collaboration with Research Software Engineers, because artefacts intended for dependable institutional or public operation continue to require expertise in security, accessibility, maintainability, integration, and sustained operation, and the method is inappropriate where contributors cannot reconstruct or assess the relation among sources, research data, maintained project knowledge, implementation, and output. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0116]] ^s107
- Promptotyping redistributes implementation labour rather than eliminating it, so evaluation must distinguish effort that is reduced from effort that is transferred, newly created, or deferred, and the combined burden of maintaining project knowledge, coordinating agentic work, and preserving a verifiable project state has not been measured systematically in the documented cases. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0117]] ^s108
- Asymmetric amplification describes the relation in which Promptotyping extends the practical reach of articulated scholarly and technical competence while its benefits remain dependent on data quality, available knowledge, access to capable systems, and the capacity to examine their outputs, so that researchers and institutions with stronger expertise, infrastructure, and verification capacity may benefit disproportionately. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0118]] ^s109
- The redistribution of labour may affect project roles unevenly, in that domain specialists may assume additional supervisory work, Research Software Engineers may inherit technical debt, and those responsible for verification may confront more plausible outputs without corresponding resources or authority. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0118]] ^s110
- The documented practice depends substantially on paid access to proprietary frontier models and capable agentic tools, proprietary dependence introduces direct costs, reduces control over system changes, and sits in tension with the inspectability, reproducibility, and durability sought for research software and data, and model and harness changes may alter system behaviour even where project knowledge and research data remain stable. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0119]] ^s111
- Promptotyping does not provide a general licence to submit research material to external generative systems, permissibility depends on the material and on applicable legal, institutional, and architectural conditions, agent access to project resources must be bounded through controlled permissions and attributable actions, and purpose-specific scholarly acceptance does not replace institutional responsibility for secure, sustainable, and legally compliant operation. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0120]] ^s112
- The documented cases do not permit claims about environmental efficiency, because the resource use of models, repeated generations, deployment architectures, and alternative workflows was not comparatively measured, and responsible use may include the decision not to apply Promptotyping to particular materials, systems, or workflows. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0121]] ^s113
- The core relation among maintained project knowledge, bounded Implementation, examination, write-back, differentiated checking, and accountable judgement is not intrinsically limited to the humanities, and Promptotyping was developed in response to a specifically humanistic difficulty profile of heterogeneous sources, interpretative modelling, incomplete evidence, and forms of adequacy that technical conformity cannot determine, so transfer would require adaptation rather than unmodified adoption. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0122]] ^s114
- Independent transfer beyond the Digital Humanities is not yet established, because a change of subject matter is insufficient where the same originating practitioner continues to organise and assess the work, and teaching cases show that participants can work with structured documents and generated implementations under guided conditions without establishing independent continuation across sustained project work. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0123]] ^s115
- Transferability must be distinguished from exact reproduction, because another practitioner may apply the method while producing a different artefact with different documents, models, tools, and project structures, and successful transfer would require the core methodological relations to be established, applied, and reconstructed without undocumented intervention by the originating scholar-developer. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0124]] ^s116
- Future evaluation should distinguish three related objects, the project knowledge base, the accepted promptotype, and Promptotyping as a socio-technical process. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0125]] ^s117
- The evaluation of the project knowledge base asks whether another contributor can reconstruct the project's current logic and limitations well enough to examine or continue the work, the evaluation of the accepted promptotype asks whether project knowledge, research data, artefact, purpose, and grounds of acceptance form a coherent and reconstructable relation, and the evaluation of the socio-technical process asks how work, intervention, learning, authority, and responsibility are distributed. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0127]] ^s118
- Bounded continuation tasks can test the practical sufficiency of a knowledge base, in that independent contributors are asked to explain the project, diagnose a selected discrepancy, or continue a delimited implementation task using the maintained documents, with observed difficulties attributed where possible to documentary, technical, access-related, or competency-related limitations. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0128]] ^s119
- Evaluation of a promptotype should distinguish technical conformity, scholarly adequacy, and suitability for the stated purpose, because an artefact may pass its tests while embodying an inadequate mapping or misleading representation, and an exploratory or proposal-stage promptotype need not satisfy production-level engineering criteria that lie outside its accepted scope. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0129]] ^s120
- Process evaluation should examine how work and responsibility are distributed, whether findings become reusable project knowledge rather than local repairs, which forms of effort are reduced, transferred, newly created, or deferred, and how much external assistance is required to maintain, verify, and continue the project. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0130]] ^s121
- Coordination should form an explicit evaluation variable, so that evaluations record dependency structure, coordination overhead, error propagation, handover volume, persistent write targets, and the distribution of checking responsibilities when they compare single-agent and multi-agent arrangements. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0131]] ^s122
- One candidate protocol separates research orchestration, which maintains scholarly questions, concepts, claims, sources, and acceptance criteria, from operational orchestration, which changes persistent artefacts and records their realised state, and its present status is a testable design hypothesis that the documented corpus does not establish as a general method rule. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0132]] ^s123
- The proposed epistemic function of functional artefacts requires comparative evaluation against source examination, specification-based methods, and established development workflows, including whether functional artefacts alter participation in modelling and design and whether the resulting decisions differ in quality, scope, or traceability. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0133]] ^s124
- Independent transfer should be evaluated through sustained project work rather than workshop completion or isolated prompting tasks, preserving successful, unsuccessful, and discontinued paths, treating model and harness as experimental variables rather than invisible background conditions, and including later handover, continuation, revision, and maintenance in the assessment. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0134]] ^s125
- Comparative evaluation should determine when maintained project knowledge, systematic write-back, differentiated checking, and versioned acceptance add value rather than seeking a universal winner, because an artefact that appears rapidly may still be difficult to examine, verify, maintain, revise, or transfer. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0135]] ^s126
- A tool operationalises only those distinctions that have been translated into its representational model, and the direction of that translation has ordinarily run from the project towards the tool, so researchers must map their data and questions into the representations and operations that existing software provides. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0136]] ^s127
- Promptotyping can partially reverse the direction of translation under stated conditions, and the promptotype is the form through which the process remains reconstructable and accountable. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0137]] ^s128
- The significance of Promptotyping is modal rather than economic, because the documented cases do not show that it produces artefacts faster, more cheaply, or more reliably than alternative approaches and instead show that forms of project-specific implementation previously outside the practical resources of individual researchers and small projects became workable at all. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0138]] ^s129
- Increasingly capable models and harnesses do not render the method obsolete, because as the volume, scope, and plausibility of delegable work grow, the span between what agents can produce and what scholars can responsibly accept widens, and maintained project knowledge, differentiated checking, write-back, and purpose-specific acceptance are the means through which that span can remain governable. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0139]] ^s130
- The paper's position is that the contribution of language-model-based agents to scholarly work is amplification rather than a transfer of authority, and that the scholarly competence and accountable judgement the method presupposes remain indispensable. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0140]] ^s131
- The paper's persistent provenance consists of the maintained project knowledge, journal, manuscript and figure sources, documented decisions, and version history preserved in the public repository, including records of the models and tools used, and these materials cannot reproduce every interaction, discarded alternative, or element of tacit judgement. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0143]] ^s132
- The author's account of an amplified writing process describes an individual experience and is not evidence that the method will work equally well for other researchers or projects. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0145]] ^s133

## Terms

- **Promptotyping**: an iterative, knowledge-driven method for developing project-specific digital research artefacts from structured research data and maintained project knowledge through Context Engineering and Agentic Engineering. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0020]]
- **Promptotype**: the accepted iteration state comprising maintained project knowledge, a referenced research-data state, the resulting artefact, and documented grounds of acceptance, coherent and identifiable for a stated purpose. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0046]]
- **Critical Expert**: the person or group competent and accountable for judging whether the project knowledge adequately represents the research material and whether the artefact is suitable for its intended purpose. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0046]]
- **Context Engineering**: the systematic selection, organisation, maintenance, and provision of the information a language-model-based system requires for its work. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0014]]
- **Agentic Engineering**: the systematic organisation of the extended, tool-supported work performed by language-model-based agents. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0015]]
- **AI harness**: the technical environment through which an agent receives context, accesses project resources, uses tools, and obtains feedback. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0016]]
- **Agentic review**: a bounded, tool-supported investigation in which agents examine outputs, data states, implementations, or artefacts against sources, references, requirements, and criteria. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0056]]
- **Write-back**: the incorporation of an interpreted finding from implementation into the maintained project state at the level where the underlying problem arises. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0104]]
- **Asymmetric amplification**: the uneven distribution of the method's benefit across roles and institutions according to expertise, infrastructure, and verification capacity. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0118]]
- **Digital research artefact**: a project-specific operational form through which researchers work with structured research data for a defined scholarly purpose. [[10_markdown/documents/promptotyping-paper#^p0007]]

## Open questions

- The paper describes an epistemic function of functional artefacts as a proposition motivated by its cases. Which parts of the book's typology of research interfaces can rest on the paper, and which are the book's own construction?
- The paper names a candidate protocol separating research orchestration from operational orchestration as a testable design hypothesis. Whether the book carries it, and with which status, is undecided.
- The paper does not describe a complete worked example of the kind Part VI announces, so the dataset and the tutorial chain of that chapter have no source in this distillate.
- The paper reports teaching cases only as insufficient evidence of independent transfer. What can be taught reliably, and where participants require technical support, is not answered here.
- The reconstructability of a research process against exact reproducibility is treated through the identifiability of the accepted state and through proprietary dependence. A definition of reconstructability as such is not given in the paper.

## Appraisal

The paper is the operator's own consolidation of a method developed in practice, and it declares its evidential limits more sharply than most methodological writing does. That declaration is what makes it usable as a source here. Its statements about the method are self-reports of a practice, so an assertion built on them carries the speaker along wherever it would otherwise assert an effect of the method rather than its description. The case sections are reconstructions from surviving documentation, which the paper itself marks as subject to selection effects, so a statement drawn from them witnesses a documented state and not a general property of the method. Where the book needs claims about comparative effectiveness, transferability, or teaching outcomes, this source deliberately withholds them, and the gap has to be filled from other material or marked as open.

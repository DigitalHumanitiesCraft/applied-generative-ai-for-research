III. Agentische Forschungsarbeit

Agentic Engineering als Arbeitsorganisation

Kapitel 9Skelett

Agentic Engineering is defined as a form of work organisation in which human activity shifts from manually performing each implementation step towards preparing knowledge, specifying goals, decomposing work, assigning permissions, inspecting intermediate results, and verifying outcomes. It includes:

  • specification before execution;
  • bounded task decomposition;
  • inspectable milestones;
  • repository-level context;
  • role and permission design;
  • the principle of least privilege;
  • single-agent and multi-agent workflows;
  • testing and visual feedback;
  • process documentation;
  • handovers;
  • and auditing.

Agentic Engineering does not imply the removal of human labour. It changes its distribution. The executing instance may be an agent, but the coherence and legitimacy of the work depend on how its environment and authority have been designed.

# The quality of a document as context is judged on length, structure, self-explanation and redundancy

## Statement

A usable document is of middling size rather than a stub or a monolith, carries frontmatter, sections and sources, explains itself without presupposing other documents, and duplicates nothing held elsewhere. The workflow that produces it runs from the identified input through the choice of the fitting transformation and its assisted execution to integration through links and map entries and a closing validation of self-containedness and substance.

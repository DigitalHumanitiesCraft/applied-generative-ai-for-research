# Pretraining and posttraining name distinguishable stages whose boundaries and terminology differ across laboratories

## Statement

Pretraining builds broad representations from large heterogeneous material, and posttraining through instruction tuning, preference learning and related methods shapes how that repertoire is expressed as assistant behaviour. Describing the first as knowledge and the second as behaviour is a simplification, because knowledge and capability are entangled throughout, and contemporary pipelines may carry intermediate stages that different laboratories name differently.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s18]] — names instruction tuning and preference learning and the resulting assistant behaviour
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e22]] — states that the knowledge-and-capability description is a simplification because both are entangled
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e23]] — states that intermediate stages exist and that boundaries and terminology differ across laboratories

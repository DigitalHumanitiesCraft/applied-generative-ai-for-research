# A public interface establishes that an implementation state existed and cannot establish its history, decisions, or authority

## Statement

A runnable artefact shows which operations it made available at the state observed. It cannot by itself establish the history of that state, the decisions that informed it, the findings written back into project knowledge, or the authority through which verification or acceptance was assigned, so claims about development, revision, checking, and acceptance remain tied to the surviving documentation.

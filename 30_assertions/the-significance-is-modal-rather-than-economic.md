# The significance of the method is modal rather than economic, in that it made bounded forms of implementation possible at all

## Statement

The documented cases do not show that Promptotyping produces artefacts faster, more cheaply, or more reliably than alternative approaches. They show that forms of project-specific implementation which had previously lain outside the practical resources of individual researchers and small projects became workable, so that requirements could be examined through functional artefacts, emerging data models could be tested against the material they were meant to organise, and proposed projects could be encountered as operational arrangements before production began.

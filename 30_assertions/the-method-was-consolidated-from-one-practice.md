# The paper presents Promptotyping as consolidated from the documented practice of one hybrid scholar-developer since 2023

## Statement

The paper reports that the method developed through language-model-supported experiments in research and teaching since 2023 and that its specification, document templates, and revision history are maintained in a public repository. It also states that the practice was led by one hybrid scholar-developer, that the cases form no controlled or representative sample, that their documentation is subject to selection effects because inspectable implementation states were preserved more systematically than abandoned experiments, and that observed improvements cannot be attributed cleanly to the method rather than to more capable systems, better tools, or increased experience.

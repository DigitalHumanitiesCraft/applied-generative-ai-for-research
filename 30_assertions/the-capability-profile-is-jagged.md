# The capability profile of a frontier model is jagged, so success on a hard task predicts little about a neighbouring easy one

## Statement

Frontier models can perform extremely difficult tasks and fail on neighbouring tasks that look simple to a human observer, which makes their competence powerful and hard to extrapolate. Outputs can carry plausible but unsupported claims, models can reproduce bias and align an answer with a belief the user has expressed, and their internal computation is only partially understood.

# The sufficiency of Distillation is practical and is tested by a new contributor or agent instance continuing the work

## Statement

A new contributor or agent instance, given the relevant knowledge documents and access to the project resources, should be able to reconstruct the project's current logic and continue the assigned work without undocumented explanation. Difficulties in doing so reveal omissions in the maintained account. Successful implementation does not establish scholarly adequacy, because a specification may be executable while resting on assumptions that cannot be justified.

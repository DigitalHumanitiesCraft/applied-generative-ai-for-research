# The knowledge base describes and contextualises sources and data rather than replacing them

## Statement

A source description is not the source and a data-model document is not the encoded file. What the holding records is the current understanding the project has of its data, its purpose and its decisions, and how the sources are to be worked with. Its point is that a statement distributed across a long guideline, several messages and oral experience becomes usable for a concrete task.

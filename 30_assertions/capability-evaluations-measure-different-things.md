# Heterogeneous capability evaluations measure different things and do not collapse into one scale

## Statement

Measurements of task duration, of adaptation to unfamiliar tasks, of mathematical reasoning and of behaviour in executable environments answer different questions. Reading them as points on a single scale of intelligence discards what each of them was built to establish.

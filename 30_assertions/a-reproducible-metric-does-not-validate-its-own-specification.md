# A deterministic metric can verify a formally specified comparison without validating the adequacy of that specification

## Statement

Character error rate can be calculated reproducibly once the reference text, the extraction rules, the normalisation, the comparison scope, and the calculation procedure have been specified. It measures deviation from a selected reference rather than objective transcription correctness, and the choice and quality of the reference, the treatment of corrections and annotations, the exclusion of particular textual phenomena, and the interpretation of the resulting value remain technical and editorial decisions.

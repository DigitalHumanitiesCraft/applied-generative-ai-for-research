# A knowledge document is a bounded, structured and revisable representation distilled from a larger holding

## Statement

It brings the statements relevant to one delimited subject together in a checkable form, so that a rule, its exceptions and its consequences can be read in one place instead of being searched again for every task. Its properties are a clearly bounded subject, a traceable structure, visible uncertainties, documented provenance, revisability, dual readability for humans and LLM-based systems, and compactness that keeps the differentiations its purpose requires.

# Data governance bounds which materials and workflows may be used, and acceptance does not replace institutional responsibility

## Statement

Promptotyping provides no general licence to submit research material to external generative systems, and permissibility depends on the material, on applicable legal and institutional conditions, and on the deployment architecture, so restricted, confidential, or personally identifiable material may require approved local processing, redaction, or exclusion. Agent access to project resources and execution environments is bounded through controlled permissions and attributable actions, consequential changes remain inspectable and reversible, and purpose-specific scholarly acceptance does not replace institutional responsibility for secure, sustainable, and legally compliant operation.

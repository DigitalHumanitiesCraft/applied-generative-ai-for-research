# Critical Expert verification resolves what the other procedures cannot determine and records who assumes responsibility

## Statement

Critical Expert verification and adjudication is the accountable examination of particular outputs against their sources and the resolution of cases that deterministic or probabilistic procedures cannot determine. It can confirm, correct, or reject previous findings, and it records who assumes responsibility for the resulting judgement.

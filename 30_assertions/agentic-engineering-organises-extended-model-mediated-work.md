# Agentic Engineering is the systematic organisation of the extended, tool-supported work an agent performs

## Statement

An AI agent is a language-model-based system that pursues a goal through a sequence of tool-supported actions and adapts to intermediate results. Agentic Engineering organises that extended work, covering how tasks are decomposed and coordinated, how agents use tools and respond to intermediate results, when human intervention is required, and how their work is inspected and continued.

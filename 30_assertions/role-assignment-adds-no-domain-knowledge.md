# Assigning a role changes terminology and perspective and adds no domain knowledge

## Statement

A role can make learned patterns of specialist communication more probable and shift style, perspective and level of detail. A brief functional assignment and an elaborated persona with background, experience, goals and situation of use should be considered together without being equated. Roles stay useful where a task genuinely requires a particular perspective, audience or evaluative frame.

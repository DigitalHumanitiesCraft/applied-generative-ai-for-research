# Interpretability findings support the claim of structured internal computation without supplying a theory that predicts prompt effects

## Statement

Attribution graphs and related procedures reconstruct parts of internal pathways and show that particular internal structures relate to observable behaviour, and interventions on representations can change outputs systematically. None of this yields a complete map from which the effect of a natural prompt could be predicted.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s50]] — states the connection between internal structures and behaviour and denies a complete predictive theory
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e51]] — names attribution graph research and the partial reconstruction of internal pathways
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e52]] — states which three claims the findings support and which they do not

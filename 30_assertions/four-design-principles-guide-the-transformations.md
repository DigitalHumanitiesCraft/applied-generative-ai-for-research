# Four design principles guide the transformations, and self-containedness is the one that decides context use

## Statement

One concept inhabits exactly one document, every document stays understandable on its own, information density is maximised per token without redundancy across documents, and provenance is secured through a sources section and frontmatter recording creation, source and status. Self-containedness is decisive for use as context, because the context a document needs has to stand inside the document rather than only in its links.

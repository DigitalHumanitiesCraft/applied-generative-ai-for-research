# AI readiness is a property of format, provenance and documented uncertainty rather than of a dataset's content

## Statement

Data are ready for use in machine learning and model-supported workflows when their formats and metadata are machine-readable, when provenance and conditions of use are clear and when quality, uncertainty and potential bias are stated. The property therefore sits in the documentation layer and complements rather than replaces broader research data principles.

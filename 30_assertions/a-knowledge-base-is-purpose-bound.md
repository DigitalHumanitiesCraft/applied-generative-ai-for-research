# A knowledge base is purpose-bound and does not aim at a complete representation

## Statement

It holds the part of the knowledge required for particular forms of work, decision and checking. Completeness is not the criterion, sufficiency for a named use is.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s80]] — states the purpose binding and denies the aim of completeness

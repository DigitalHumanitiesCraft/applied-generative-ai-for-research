# The documented cases permit no claim about environmental efficiency, and responsible use may mean not applying the method

## Statement

The resource use of models, repeated generations, deployment architectures, and alternative workflows was not comparatively measured, so reduced development effort is no evidence of reduced resource consumption. Responsible use requires proportionate system selection and may include the decision not to apply the method to particular materials, systems, or workflows.

# Process evaluation follows how work and responsibility are distributed and whether findings become reusable knowledge

## Statement

Process evaluation determines which forms of effort are reduced, transferred, newly created, or deferred, how much external assistance is required to maintain, verify, and continue the project, and whether findings become reusable project knowledge rather than remaining local repairs to a single implementation state.

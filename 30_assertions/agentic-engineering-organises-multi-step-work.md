# Agentic Engineering is the systematic organisation and control of multi-step agentic work

## Statement

It covers the bounding and decomposition of tasks, tool use, the processing of intermediate results, states and handovers, conditions for abort and escalation, and checking and continuation. Its central question is under which conditions the actions of an agent stay traceable, bounded and correctable, and its object reaches beyond code to data descriptions, specifications, mappings, design decisions, process documents and verification concepts.

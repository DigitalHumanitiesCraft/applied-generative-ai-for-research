# A vision language model produces errors that are contextually plausible rather than locally obvious

## Statement

A general-purpose multimodal model can receive a facsimile with an instruction and return a transcription without being a dedicated recognition system, because visual patterns, layout, linguistic context and the instruction jointly shape the output. That joint conditioning creates the characteristic failure, a reading that fits the sentence and not the page. Whether the resulting ability on unseen handwriting counts as emergent depends on the definition, on scale and on training data that is not disclosed.

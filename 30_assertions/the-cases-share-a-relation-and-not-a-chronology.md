# The cases share a reconstructable relation among knowledge, data state, artefact, findings, and judgement rather than a chronology

## Statement

The cases begin from different project states and foreground different functions of implementation, and each maintains a reconstructable relation among maintained project knowledge, a referenced research-data state, the resulting artefact, findings arising from its examination, and responsible judgement. Promptotyping therefore prescribes neither a single project chronology nor one artefact form.

# The proposed epistemic function of functional artefacts requires comparison against methods that do not build one

## Statement

Studies should compare findings emerging through implementation with those produced through source examination, specification-based methods, and established development workflows, examine whether functional artefacts alter participation in modelling and design, ask what forms of expertise remain necessary, and test whether the resulting decisions differ in quality, scope, or traceability.

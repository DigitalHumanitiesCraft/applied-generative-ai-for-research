# Prompt effects are local to model, task, language, position and metric, and they do not generalise into rules

## Statement

Studies report effects of emotional additions, of politeness and of unusual automatically generated prompts, while other work shows that irrelevant additions degrade performance or that effects fail to replicate on newer models. A changed result after a variation does not show that the wording works for the reason assumed, and effects found for one generation of models may weaken, disappear or reverse for another.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s43]] — reports the heterogeneous findings on emotional additions, politeness and eccentric prompts
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s44]] — lists the six factors on which prompt effects depend

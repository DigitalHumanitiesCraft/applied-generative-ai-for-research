# The maintained account of a project must be proportionate to the scope and consequences of the work being delegated

## Statement

A bounded transformation operating on a stable and well-understood format may be guided by concise mappings and formal checks. Work spanning interpretation, modelling, implementation, and verification requires a more explicit account of the project's sources, assumptions, requirements, and limitations.

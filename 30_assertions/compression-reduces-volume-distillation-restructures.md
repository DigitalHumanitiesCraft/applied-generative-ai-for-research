# Compression reduces the volume of a context while distillation restructures an understanding

## Statement

Selection of sections, summary, removal of repetition, aggregation and compaction of a working history all reduce what is supplied, and a shorter version is not automatically a better one, because a summary can smooth uncertainty, remove justifications or turn several alternatives into an apparently unambiguous rule. Distillation transfers available understanding into a selective, structured, inspectable and revisable representation intended to be sufficient for further work.

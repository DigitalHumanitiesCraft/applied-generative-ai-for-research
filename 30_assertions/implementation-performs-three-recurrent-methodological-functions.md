# Implementation performs three recurrent methodological functions, production and curation, prospective modelling, and project formation

## Statement

Production and curation make the operations for creating, transforming, checking, correcting, and exporting research data functional and inspectable, and their significance lies also in making the route from source material to differentiated output and checking states explicit. Prospective modelling and exploration use functional artefacts to examine an emerging model or the project-specific consequences of an existing representation, so that modelling and representation decisions can be assessed through the data to which they apply. Project formation and bounded communication give functional form to a proposed scholarly arrangement before production begins, with the accepted purpose of making the proposal available for examination and communication.

# A project-specific artefact remains selective and allows its selections to be examined against the project's own data and purpose

## Statement

A project-specific artefact does not escape selection. What it changes is where the selection is made and whether it can be inspected, because the choices are taken in relation to the project's own data and scholarly purpose and remain available for examination there.

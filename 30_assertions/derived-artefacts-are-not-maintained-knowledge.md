# Derived project artefacts are regenerable outputs and remain distinct from maintained project knowledge

## Statement

A derived project artefact is produced from a referenced project state through an identified process and stored in a format appropriate to its purpose, with its provenance and status recorded. It records a derived observation rather than an interpretation or decision that project contributors have examined and adopted, which is what keeps it outside the maintained knowledge.

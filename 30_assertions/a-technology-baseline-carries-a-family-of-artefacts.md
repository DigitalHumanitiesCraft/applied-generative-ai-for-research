# A technology baseline carries the technology decisions of a whole family of artefacts and is referenced rather than repeated

## Statement

A baseline is published centrally so that a project instance references it, and the architecture document of that instance records only the project-specific stack and the deviations from the baseline. Its rules bind how an artefact of the family is built, which is why it counts as an action document despite being written as reasoned rules.

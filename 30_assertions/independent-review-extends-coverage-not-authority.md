# Independent review extends the coverage of checking and creates no truth by agreement

## Statement

Several reviewers can expose disagreement and locate suspicious cases, and the evidence returned by schemas, tests, source comparisons and domain knowledge stays more important than agreement among them. The purpose of orchestrating several instances is a structured trajectory of independent work and feedback rather than a larger number of model calls.

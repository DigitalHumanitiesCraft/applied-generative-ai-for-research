# The tendency to agree with the user has to be answered in the procedure rather than in the wording

## Statement

Where agreement with a stated belief could bias a result, the procedure requires evidence, alternatives or independent critical review. Producing independent candidates, inspecting their differences and adjudicating between them is the corresponding operation.

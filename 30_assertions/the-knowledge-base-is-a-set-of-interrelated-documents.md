# The project knowledge base consists of interrelated documents whose three types are organisational heuristics rather than a prescribed file structure

## Statement

The documents of the knowledge base are bounded representations distilled from fuller research material, maintained for human inspection and revision, and made available for inclusion in the task-specific working contexts of agents. Their three types are heuristics, so projects may name, combine, and distribute their functions as their work requires.

# Each of the five transformations has a precursor in documentation science or knowledge management

## Statement

Compression corresponds to abstracting, codified as theory and technique and normed as a procedure in an international standard. Normalisation corresponds to formal description according to a rule set. Enrichment corresponds to subject indexing and semantic description. Consolidation corresponds to information consolidation, which separates restructuring of content from repackaging of form, and to the combination mode of a knowledge creation model. Atomisation corresponds to the monographic principle of documentation and to a card-index practice with atomic, fixed-address, cross-linked units. The correspondences rest on a web-supported literature check that the source records with its date.

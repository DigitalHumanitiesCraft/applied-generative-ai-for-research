# Knowledge Engineering builds the holding and Context Engineering assembles from it what a task needs

## Statement

The two activities answer different questions, what the project knows and what the agent needs now, and the second presupposes the first, because only a structured holding permits selective loading. That dependence is what separates Context Engineering from a better kind of prompting.

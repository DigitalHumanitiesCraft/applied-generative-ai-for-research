# Language models reduce parts of the implementation effort, which makes provisional artefacts available as instruments of specification

## Statement

Language models can reduce the effort of mapping research data to the models existing software expects and of implementing more closely adapted ways of working with those data, while any step that first has to recognise or interpret a structure remains probabilistic. Because not all relevant requirements can be determined in advance, provisional artefacts can reveal problems in the specification and show what the data can and cannot support, so implementation becomes part of determining how researchers wish to work with their material.

# Context Engineering is the systematic selection, organisation, maintenance and provision of the information state of a task

## Statement

It determines which information, instructions, tools and examples are available at a given moment, in which form and order they are supplied, when further material is loaded and what deliberately stays outside.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s56]] — defines Context Engineering and names form, order, later loading and deliberate omission
- [[20_distillates/documents/slide-deck#^k3]] — states that the object is the information state of a concrete task
- [[20_distillates/documents/slide-deck#^k10]] — gives the compact definition of selection, organisation and provision

# The core relation is not intrinsically limited to the humanities, and transfer would require adaptation rather than adoption

## Statement

The relation among maintained project knowledge, bounded implementation, examination, write-back, differentiated checking, and accountable judgement could in principle apply wherever structured research data must be translated into a project-specific computational form. The method was nevertheless developed against a specifically humanistic difficulty profile of heterogeneous historical and cultural sources, interpretative modelling, incomplete or uncertain evidence, and forms of adequacy that technical conformity cannot determine. Fields with more standardised data structures may encounter a less demanding version of the problem, and other domains may require statistical, regulatory, or safety procedures the documented practice does not represent.

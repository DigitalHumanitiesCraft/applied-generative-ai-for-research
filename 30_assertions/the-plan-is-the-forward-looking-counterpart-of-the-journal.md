# The plan orders outstanding work into milestones with exit conditions anchored against the specification

## Statement

The plan is delimited against its neighbours by direction and by subject. The provenance record is retrospective and is never rewritten, while the plan is prospective and is carried forward; the specification says what is built and why, while the plan says when and in which order. An exit condition states how completion is recognised, is anchored against an acceptance criterion, a quality gate, or a decision of the specification, and is formulated as a done-when statement rather than as a declaration of intent.

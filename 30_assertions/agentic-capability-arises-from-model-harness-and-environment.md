# Agentic capability arises from the combined model, harness, and environment rather than from the model alone

## Statement

An AI harness is the technical environment through which an agent receives context, accesses project resources, uses tools, and obtains feedback, providing controlled access to files, execution environments, tests, and running artefacts while maintaining state and permissions. Results therefore depend on the information available to the system, the organisation of the work, and that technical environment, beyond the choice of model and the formulation of a prompt.

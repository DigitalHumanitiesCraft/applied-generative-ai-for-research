# The granularity a holding needs for a human reader and for a model context may differ, and the conflict is unresolved

## Statement

A knowledge model built for the context of a language model and one built for human reading can demand different granularity, and the possible conflict between compact machine readability and detailed human traceability is not settled. How the quality of a knowledge model could be measured at all is equally open.

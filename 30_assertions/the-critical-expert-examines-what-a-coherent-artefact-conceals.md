# The Critical Expert examines whether alternatives were excluded, conventions reproduced, or absences concealed by an apparently coherent artefact

## Statement

Maintained project knowledge guides Implementation without determining a single adequate realisation, because natural-language descriptions retain ambiguity and different agent runs may realise the same requirement in materially different ways. The responsibility of the role therefore extends beyond detecting errors in generated content to examining whether relevant alternatives were excluded, whether conventions were reproduced without justification, and whether absences were concealed by an artefact that looks coherent.

# Self-revision by the producing model makes errors visible and is no independent verification

## Statement

The same model can overlook its own false assumptions or justify them plausibly after the fact. Self-revision becomes more reliable where explicit criteria, external tests and checkable references are available, which shifts the reliability into the surrounding arrangement rather than into the revision itself.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s40]] — states that self-revision is no independent verification and names what makes it more reliable

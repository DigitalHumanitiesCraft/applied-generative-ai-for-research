# Independent transfer is evaluated through sustained project work rather than through workshop completion

## Statement

New domain contributors should receive the method specification, the relevant project materials, and access to an appropriate technical environment, and should then develop and assess a bounded promptotype without continuing intervention by the originating scholar-developer. The evaluation preserves successful, unsuccessful, and discontinued paths, records changes to project knowledge, artefacts, human intervention, external assistance, and grounds of acceptance, treats model and harness as experimental variables rather than invisible background conditions, and includes later handover, continuation, revision, and maintenance.

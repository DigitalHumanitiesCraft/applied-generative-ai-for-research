# A promptotype is the accepted iteration state in which project knowledge, data state, artefact, and grounds of acceptance form one identifiable whole

## Statement

A Promptotyping iteration concludes when the maintained project knowledge, the resulting digital research artefact, the referenced research-data state, and the documented grounds of acceptance form a coherent and identifiable state for a stated purpose. A runnable or plausible artefact alone does not meet this threshold if its relation to the project knowledge, the data, and the grounds of acceptance cannot be reconstructed.

# The recurring structure of Promptotyping lies in the relation between maintained knowledge and an artefact, and no software form is constitutive

## Statement

Each promptotype gives functional consequences to a bounded account of the project's data, purpose, requirements, and conditions of assessment. What recurs is that relation between maintained project knowledge and an artefact that makes selected scholarly operations available for examination, and not any particular technology or artefact form.

# Deterministic verification tests conformity to formalised requirements and concludes nothing beyond the properties its checks encode

## Statement

Schemas, constraints, transformation tests, structural audits, and reproducible measurements decide whether formally expressible conditions are satisfied. The conclusions of such a check are limited to the properties the check encodes and do not establish that the requirements themselves are adequate.

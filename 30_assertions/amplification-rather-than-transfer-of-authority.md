# The contribution of model-based agents to scholarly work is amplification, and authority stays where responsibility lies

## Statement

Promptotyping can partially reverse the direction in which research data are translated into computational form, and the promptotype is the form through which that process remains reconstructable and accountable. Within the declared evidential scope the position is that the contribution of language-model-based agents is amplification rather than a transfer of authority, and that the scholarly competence and accountable judgement the method presupposes remain indispensable.

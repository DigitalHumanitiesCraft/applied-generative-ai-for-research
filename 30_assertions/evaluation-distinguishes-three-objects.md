# A future evaluation of the method distinguishes the knowledge base, the accepted promptotype, and the socio-technical process

## Statement

The evaluation of the project knowledge base asks whether another contributor can reconstruct the project's current logic and limitations well enough to examine or continue the work, and looks to maintained documents, decision rationales, examples, known limitations, and unresolved questions. The evaluation of the accepted promptotype asks whether project knowledge, research data, artefact, purpose, and grounds of acceptance form a coherent and reconstructable relation, and looks to the referenced data state, the versioned implementation, the checks, the source comparison, the expert assessment, and the acceptance record. The evaluation of the socio-technical process asks how work, intervention, learning, authority, and responsibility are distributed.

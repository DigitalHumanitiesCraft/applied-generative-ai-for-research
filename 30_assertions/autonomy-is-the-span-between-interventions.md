# Autonomy is the span of work between two human interventions and not the absence of control

## Statement

Reading autonomy as a quantity of freedom misses what the term measures in this practice. It measures how much work happens before a person looks again, which is a design decision about the arrangement rather than a property of the model.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s110]] — defines autonomy as the extent of work between two interventions and denies the absence of control

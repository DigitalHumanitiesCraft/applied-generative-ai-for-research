# The precision of a prompt does not follow from its length

## Statement

A very long prompt can be contradictory or hard to prioritise, and a short one suffices where the relevant rules already stand in knowledge documents and instruction files. A bounded formulation reduces the number of silent assumptions without guaranteeing a correct result.

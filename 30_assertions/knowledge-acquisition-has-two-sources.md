# Knowledge acquisition draws on existing documents and data and on the implicit knowledge of persons and organisations

## Statement

Existing material is prepared and converted into machine-readable and distilled forms, and knowledge that stands in no document is raised through document analysis, interviews, workshops, observation of workflows, error analysis and joint modelling and brought into the same structured form. Implicit knowledge covers the reasons for earlier decisions, known exceptions, expectations of the target artefact, acceptance criteria and practical experience with particular source types, and a rule practised for years may appear in no guideline until an interview surfaces it.

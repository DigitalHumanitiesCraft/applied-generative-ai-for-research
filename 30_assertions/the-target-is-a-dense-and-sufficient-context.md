# The target of context work is a dense and sufficient context rather than a short one

## Statement

Too strong a reduction removes conditions, uncertainties and provenance and makes the result differently bad. What is wanted is as bounded as possible and as complete and differentiated as the task requires, every statement carrying information, with a minimal core permanently present and depth loaded on demand.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s61]] — states the target and the risk of over-reduction
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e77]] — gives the principle of the right information in the right representation at the right time

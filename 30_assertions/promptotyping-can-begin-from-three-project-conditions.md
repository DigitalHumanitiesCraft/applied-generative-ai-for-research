# Promptotyping can begin from an established data state, participate in developing that state, or operationalise a prospective arrangement

## Statement

Some documented projects already possessed structured data, established models, and ongoing production workflows. Others began with an emerging model, a limited sample, provisional requirements, or a proposal-stage description of a future project. The method therefore has no single entry condition.

# A project governance document records authority, permissions, evidential status, rights, write-back targets, and escalation paths

## Statement

Projects whose agents act across several persistent resources or handle consequential claims, rights, and release decisions may maintain a versioned governance document. Its purpose is to make the conditions of agentic action inspectable without embedding them in temporary task instructions, and the need for it depends on the workflow.

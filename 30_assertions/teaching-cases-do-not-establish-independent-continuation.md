# Teaching cases show guided work with structured documents and establish no independent continuation across sustained project work

## Statement

Independent transfer beyond the field in which the method arose is not established, because a change of subject matter is insufficient where the same originating practitioner continues to organise and assess the work. Teaching cases show that participants can work with structured documents and generated implementations under guided conditions, and they do not show that participants continue such work independently over a sustained project.

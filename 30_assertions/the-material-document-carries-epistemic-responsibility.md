# The material document makes transparent what the data support and where they stop carrying

## Statement

The material document addresses a reviewer who wants to judge data quality, a coding agent that is to process or transform the data, and a domain expert who wants to follow the selection logic. Per source it records origin, capture logic, licence, provenance, and capture period, and where several sources are used it explains the relation between them.

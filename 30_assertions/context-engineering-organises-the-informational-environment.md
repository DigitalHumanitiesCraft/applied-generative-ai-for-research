# Context Engineering is the systematic selection, organisation, maintenance, and provision of the information a model-based system requires

## Statement

Context Engineering extends Prompt Engineering from individual prompts to the wider informational environment in which they are interpreted. It covers the selection, organisation, maintenance, and provision of what a language-model-based system needs for its work, and it does not consist in placing all available material into a context window.

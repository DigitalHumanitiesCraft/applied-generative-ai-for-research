# The structure of a knowledge holding carries meaning, so every structural decision is an epistemic one

## Statement

A holding of plain-text notes is a materialised semantic net in which links, tags and hierarchies themselves carry meaning and can be fed selectively into the context of a model. A link that is set, a tag that is chosen and a division of a document into smaller units decide how the knowledge can later be interpreted by a human reader and by a model alike, because they fix which connections are visible, which concepts count as independent and how the net behaves under selective loading.

# A bounded demonstrator can make a proposed scholarly arrangement examinable before a production corpus and workflow exist

## Statement

Promptotyping can support project formation before a complete corpus, production workflow, or funded development process exists, by making a proposed editorial and technical arrangement functional enough to be examined and communicated within the preparation of a funding proposal. In the documented case, Distillation translated the emerging editorial and organisational account into requirements and design decisions, and Implementation produced a bounded path from the initial material to an encoded representation and from that representation to a statically hosted Edition Interface. The resulting artefact allowed the planned project to be encountered as a functional arrangement rather than only as a textual description.

# Technical verification and scholarly validation answer different questions, so a correct implementation may still be inadequate

## Statement

Technical verification assesses whether an output or implementation conforms to formalised requirements such as a schema, a test, a constraint, or a specified mapping. Scholarly validation assesses whether the underlying representation is warranted by the source material and adequate for its intended research purpose. An implementation may therefore be technically correct while failing scholarly validation, because adequacy depends on the interpretation of sources, the modelling decisions through which they are represented, and the purpose the resulting data serve.

# Separating research orchestration from operational orchestration is a testable design hypothesis rather than a rule of the method

## Statement

One candidate protocol separates research orchestration, which maintains scholarly questions, concepts, claims, sources, and acceptance criteria, from operational orchestration, which changes persistent artefacts and records their realised state. A versioned brief can convey accepted scholarly commitments to implementation, and a report can return checks, deviations, and new findings for write-back. The documented corpus does not establish this arrangement as a general method rule.

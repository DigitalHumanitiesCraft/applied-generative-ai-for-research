# Agents and validators assemble evidence and do not thereby acquire the authority to accept a result

## Statement

They can localise errors, apply criteria, report differences and collect evidence, and none of that transfers the decision. Expertise is not removed by this arrangement, it moves to defining purposes, externalising knowledge, establishing modelling distinctions, setting constraints, designing evaluation criteria and deciding how evidence affects the status of an output.

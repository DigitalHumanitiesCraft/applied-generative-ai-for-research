## Open questions

- Which dataset carries the worked example, and is it registered as a source? The paper describes no tutorial project, so the concrete chain of the worked-example chapter has no anchor in this topic.
- The paper treats reconstructability through the identifiability of the accepted state and through proprietary dependence, and it gives no definition of reconstructability as such. The definition the outline announces is therefore the book's own until a source carries it.
- The hands-on chains of the lecture notes and the slide deck feed Part VI and belong to the other writing lane, so the teaching form of the worked example is not anchored here.
- Which archival or publication strategy the book recommends for a promptotype is not settled by the sources, which name durable reference without prescribing a form.

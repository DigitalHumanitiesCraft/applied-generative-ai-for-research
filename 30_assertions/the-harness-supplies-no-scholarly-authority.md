# The harness supplies technical control and decides nothing about scholarly adequacy

## Statement

A validator can establish that an element is admissible at a position and cannot decide whether the source is actually illegible or whether another reading is more probable. The same holds one level up. The environment can bound and record what happens, and it does not determine which project knowledge is relevant or whether a modelling decision is adequate.

# Filing information into a holding is modelling work rather than storage

## Statement

Reading the holding as a knowledge model turns every operation on it into a modelling operation that actively forms a model of one's own knowledge in the act of filing. Three consequences follow for practice, that structural maintenance is modelling, that the holding becomes an external memory which can be loaded into contexts, and that new knowledge arises above all through the transformations that produce its units.

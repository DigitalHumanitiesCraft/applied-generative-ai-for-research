# Distillation moves from a case through patterns to a principle that holds without the case

## Statement

The staged logic runs from a concrete case through the extraction of patterns and their abstraction into principles detached from the single instance to a condensation into a storable and retrievable form. The result applies independently of the originating example, so a reader can transfer the principle without having seen the material it came from. A document of this kind stores thinking tools rather than facts, and the test of a successful one is whether someone who reads only the document can apply the principle to a new case without asking questions.

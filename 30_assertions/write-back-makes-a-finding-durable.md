# Write-back is the mechanism through which a finding from implementation becomes durable project knowledge

## Statement

A correction becomes methodologically consequential when it is incorporated into maintained project knowledge rather than confined to the current implementation, which allows subsequent work to proceed from the revised understanding. Write-back is therefore not an additional phase. Its purpose is to incorporate the interpreted consequence of a finding into the maintained project state rather than to preserve every observation, and not every consequential finding requires revision, because implementation may instead establish the boundary of what an iteration can responsibly claim.

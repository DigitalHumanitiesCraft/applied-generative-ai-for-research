# Problems in the organisation of agentic work, checking, and authority are corrected in the arrangement rather than in the research domain

## Statement

Where a finding concerns how agentic work is organised, how it is checked, or how authority is allocated, the correction changes working contexts, permissions, escalation procedures, agent instructions, verification states, or acceptance criteria, and it leaves the represented research domain untouched.

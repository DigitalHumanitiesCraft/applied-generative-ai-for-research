# The status report is the one document type of the method whose addressee is external to the project

## Statement

The report answers a third party without prior knowledge of the repository what was done in the reporting period, where the project stands on the reporting date, which robust results are available, and what happens next. It is separated from the provenance record by lifecycle and degree of curation rather than by content, in that the record carries continuous chronological entries for internal addressees while the report is a single editorially condensed state without a narrative of the course of work. The report quotes from the record, and the record never references the report.

# A knowledge document is transferable, compact and retrievable, and it is neither textbook nor tutorial

## Statement

Its format rests on dual readability, compactness with maximal information at minimal token cost, and portability as a plain-text file that can be linked and exchanged. Transferability means the knowledge stays applicable to situations unknown when it was written, compactness that only what the application needs is present, retrievability that headings, metadata and structure make it quickly reactivatable. It presupposes a reader who knows the context or can work it out, and it delivers the concentrated principle.

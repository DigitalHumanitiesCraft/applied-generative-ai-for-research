# Distillation runs through selection, structuring and condensation

## Statement

Selection decides what is relevant to the subject, structuring makes terms, rules and relations explicit, and condensation removes redundancy without losing necessary differentiation. What has to survive are relevant terms and distinctions, relations and dependencies, conditions and constraints, uncertainties and open questions, and the justifications behind decisions.

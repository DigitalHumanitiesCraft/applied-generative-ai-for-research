# The method can articulate and revise available understanding and cannot supply domain knowledge a project does not have

## Statement

Templates and maintained documents may make assumptions more explicit and revisable, and they cannot compensate for missing competence, because the method supports the articulation, implementation, and revision of understanding that already exists. Inadequate project knowledge can guide an agent towards an apparently coherent and operational implementation that remains inadequate in scholarly terms, so responsible application requires critical examination of the provenance, construction, scope, and representational limits of the data.

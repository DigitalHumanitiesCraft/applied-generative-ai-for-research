# The specification holds requirements, scenarios, functional scope, and decisions together because their answers are interlocked

## Statement

A story motivates a requirement, a feature implements it, and a decision justifies why that feature is built as it is. Separating the layers into different documents risks a change in one layer silently ageing the others, which is why the specification answers the four questions in one place.

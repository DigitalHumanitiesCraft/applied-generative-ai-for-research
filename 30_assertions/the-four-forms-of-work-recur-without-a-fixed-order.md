# Preparation, Exploration, Distillation, and Implementation are recurrent forms of work rather than a fixed sequence

## Statement

The four phases are analytically distinct and do not form a fixed or exclusively linear sequence. Their depth, order, and recurrence depend on the state and purpose of the project, and findings arising from Implementation may return the work to any earlier phase, most frequently to Distillation, where the maintained project knowledge is revised.

# Capture, transformation, exploration, edition, verification, and integrated workbenches are the recurring operational forms of a promptotype

## Statement

Capture makes a data model actionable during structured data creation. Transformation maps source or research data into another explicit representation. Exploration supports investigation, comparison, filtering, and navigation. Edition makes an editorial account of texts and their relations operational through transformation, presentation, navigation, and annotation. Verification supports the inspection of outputs, evidence, checking procedures, and differentiated states. Integrated scholarly workbenches combine several of these forms for a bounded research task, and a single promptotype may combine them.

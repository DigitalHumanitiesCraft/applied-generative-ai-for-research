# Verification is delimited against quality assurance and against the provenance record by object and by time

## Statement

Quality assurance examines system behaviour against the specification, while verification examines whether substantive claims are covered by the raw data. The provenance record holds the chronological course of decisions, while verification is the synchronous, antagonistic examination of one claim against its evidence. A verification names the units under examination and the reference standard against which they are held, such as statements against character-exact full texts or transcriptions against verified references.

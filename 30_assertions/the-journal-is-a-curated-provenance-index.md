# The provenance record is a curated index of transitions and carries neither current status nor open tasks

## Statement

The record evidences transitions that belong together in substance, while current contents remain in the documents responsible for them. An entry arises per such transition and records whether the result was integrated, discarded, or corrected, and the record carries no current project status, no open tasks, and no detailed check results. It is condensed when repetition, copied durable knowledge, completed open lists, scattered reasons for decisions, or an over-expensive regular reading context impair its provenance function, and neither its length nor the number of its entries triggers condensation on its own.

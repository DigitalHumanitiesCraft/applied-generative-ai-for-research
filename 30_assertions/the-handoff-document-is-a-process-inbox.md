# The process inbox carries open handover points alone, and processing a point ends with its removal

## Statement

The presence of a point in the inbox means that it is open, and an empty inbox remains a valid state of the document. Processing integrates durable content first into the responsible declarative or action document, then writes a short entry in the provenance record naming subject, source, target, and result or reason for rejection, and then removes the point completely. A dated exceptional handover state may be kept beside the inbox as a frozen record without replacing it, and a durable research-to-operations contract is kept as an integration document instead.

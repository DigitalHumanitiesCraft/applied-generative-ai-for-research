# Context Engineering and Agentic Engineering are interdependent, and neither compensates for a failure of the other

## Statement

Context Engineering organises the task-specific information and project access available to an agent, while Agentic Engineering structures how the agent acts within those conditions. A carefully maintained knowledge base does not determine an adequate course of action, and a well-organised agentic workflow cannot compensate for an inadequate account of the data or the research purpose.

# More capable models widen the span between what agents can produce and what scholars can responsibly accept

## Statement

As the volume, scope, and plausibility of delegable work grow, the distance between generated output and responsible acceptance grows with it. Maintained project knowledge, differentiated checking, write-back, and purpose-specific acceptance are the means through which that span can remain governable, and their importance increases with the capabilities they organise and constrain.

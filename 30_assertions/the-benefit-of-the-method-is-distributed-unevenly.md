# Asymmetric amplification names the uneven distribution of the method's benefit across roles and institutions

## Statement

Promptotyping may extend the practical reach of articulated scholarly and technical competence, while its benefits remain dependent on data quality, available knowledge, access to capable systems, and the capacity to examine their outputs, so researchers and institutions with stronger expertise, infrastructure, and verification capacity may benefit disproportionately. The same redistribution affects project roles unevenly, in that domain specialists may assume additional supervisory work, Research Software Engineers may inherit technical debt, and those responsible for verification may confront more plausible outputs without corresponding resources or authority.

# Public statements by frontier laboratories about automating research are self-descriptions of an objective

## Statement

Leading figures of frontier AI companies have described the automation of research as a strategic goal. Such a statement records the direction in which a laboratory is attempting to extend capability, and it is not evidence that research has been automated.

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e11]] — reports the public statements and separates the stated objective from evidence of achievement

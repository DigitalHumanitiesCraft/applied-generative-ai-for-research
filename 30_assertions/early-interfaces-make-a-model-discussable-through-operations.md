# Early interfaces let project partners examine a model through entering, displaying, filtering, and comparing rather than through schemas alone

## Statement

Rapidly developed interfaces gave project partners a shared operational environment in which the emerging model could be exercised, while its categories, relations, and capture requirements stayed open to revision. The model was thereby discussed through the records assigned to it, the relations it permitted, the information it required during capture, and the representations it supported.

# Acceptance rests with the Critical Expert, who specifies and revises the project knowledge and verifies the artefact

## Statement

The Critical Expert is the person or group competent and accountable for judging whether the project knowledge adequately represents the research material and whether the artefact is suitable for its intended purpose. The role specifies and revises project knowledge, verifies the artefact, and accepts the iteration for its stated purpose, thereby establishing an identifiable promptotype. An agent may contribute proposals and assessments and cannot assume responsibility for their adequacy.

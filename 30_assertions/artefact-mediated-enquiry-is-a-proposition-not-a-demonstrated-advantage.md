# The claim that functional artefacts improve scholarly enquiry remains a conceptual proposition motivated by the cases

## Statement

The documented practice does not establish that functional artefacts broaden participation, improve decisions, or produce findings that could not also have emerged through source criticism, specification review, data profiling, non-functional prototypes, or structured discussion. Artefact-mediated scholarly enquiry is therefore a proposition the cases motivate rather than a demonstrated comparative advantage.

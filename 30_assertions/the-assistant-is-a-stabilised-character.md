# The assistant encountered in an interface is a behavioural pattern stabilised by training, instructions and context

## Statement

The figure that answers is not the underlying network and not a human counterpart. Providers stabilise it through training, runtime instructions, policy layers and product design. This explains why matter-of-fact structured communication is often productive and why the coherence of the exchange says nothing about intentionality or authority in the matter.

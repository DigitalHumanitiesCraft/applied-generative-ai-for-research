# Evaluating a promptotype separates technical conformity, scholarly adequacy, and suitability for the stated purpose

## Statement

Tests and schemas establish conformity to formalised requirements, while source comparison and responsible expert judgement are required where fidelity and interpretative adequacy are at stake. An artefact may pass its tests while embodying an inadequate mapping or a misleading representation, and an exploratory or proposal-stage promptotype need not satisfy production-level engineering criteria that lie outside its accepted scope.

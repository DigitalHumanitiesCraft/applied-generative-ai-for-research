# Software participates in scholarly knowledge production rather than serving as a neutral means of access

## Statement

Digital tool criticism has shown that a tool shapes which modelled distinctions become visible and what researchers can do with them. Generic tools provide predefined representations and forms of interaction that may not correspond to a project's data and scholarly practices, so researchers may have to adapt their inquiry to assumptions developed for other purposes.

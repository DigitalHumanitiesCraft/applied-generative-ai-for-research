# Declarative knowledge documents state what the project takes to be the case about its material and what the artefact is required to realise

## Statement

Declarative documents cover the structure, semantics, provenance, gaps, and uncertainties of the research data, the connection between scholarly practices and expected system behaviour, and the way modelled distinctions should be represented in the artefact. In the template set the material document carries the epistemic responsibility for what the data are, where they come from, how they are modelled, and where they stop carrying, while the specification document consolidates requirements, epics and user stories, functional scope, and decisions so that the what and the why of the project are answered in one place.

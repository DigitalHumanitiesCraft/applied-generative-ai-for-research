# The form of Exploration follows the scale of the material, so large collections are profiled computationally

## Statement

A single text or a small collection may be examined directly, including through a language model where appropriate, while large or heterogeneous collections require computational profiling, for example code that extracts and aggregates structural information across thousands of encoded documents. The collection remains within the project environment while the resulting profiles and selected examples enter the agent's working context.

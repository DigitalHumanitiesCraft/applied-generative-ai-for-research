# Where interaction logs are not retained, the persistent provenance of a generated process is its documentary and version record

## Statement

The persistent provenance of the documented writing process consists of the maintained project knowledge, the working record, the manuscript and figure sources, the documented decisions, and the version history preserved in the public repository, including records of the models and tools used. These materials make consequential stages inspectable, and they cannot reproduce every interaction, discarded alternative, or element of tacit judgement.

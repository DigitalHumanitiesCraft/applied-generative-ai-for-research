# The maintained knowledge of the documented workflows preserved uncertainty and negative findings rather than settling them

## Statement

The knowledge directories of the two production workflows record characteristics of the source material, processing requirements, annotation and checking conventions, methodological decisions, unresolved questions, and reasons for revising earlier approaches, including phenomena that the available reference material does not represent and therefore cannot support as a basis for comparison. Distillation there preserved uncertainty and negative findings instead of converting incomplete evidence into apparently settled requirements.

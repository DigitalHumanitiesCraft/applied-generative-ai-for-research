# The Critical Expert designs and maintains the conditions of interpretation rather than checking every output by hand

## Statement

The role carries the judgment where checking requires knowledge of the sources, interpretation or design decision, and it decides which reading is defensible, whether a modelling corresponds to the source, whether the surface preserves scholarly distinctions and whether a state is accepted for its purpose. It is the responsible authority in the project rather than a particular person, and its work includes provenance, validation rules, acceptance criteria and procedures for handling uncertainty.

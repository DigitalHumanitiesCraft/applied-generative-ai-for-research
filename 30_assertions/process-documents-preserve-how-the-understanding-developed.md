# Process documents preserve how the project's understanding developed, backwards as provenance and forwards as sequence

## Statement

Process documents record attempted approaches, failures, unresolved questions, and the reasons for consequential decisions, and together with version control and informative commit messages they make changes and their rationale traceable. The template set splits the type by direction, keeping a retrospective carrier that is never rewritten and a prospective carrier that orders outstanding work into phases and milestones with entry and exit conditions anchored against the specification.

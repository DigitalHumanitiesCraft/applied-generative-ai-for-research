# Requirements arise from the relation among data, research question and scholarly practice

## Statement

Capture, curation, understanding and exploration need different representations and working contexts, so an environment for this work cannot be reduced to a single prompt or one static database. Aligning development with scholarly practice does not mean implementing every stated preference, its purpose is to make the relation between questions, data affordances and digital operations explicit enough to be inspected and negotiated.

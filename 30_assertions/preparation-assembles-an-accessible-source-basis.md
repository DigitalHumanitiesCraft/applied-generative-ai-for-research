# Preparation assembles the source basis and research context and requires accessibility and documentation rather than normalisation

## Statement

Preparation brings the relevant research material into an accessible project environment and makes its current status explicit, without requiring that material to be normalised, formally modelled, or placed directly in an agent's working context. It also gathers the scholar's current understanding and the project's provisional purpose as documented in existing working materials. It is provisionally sufficient when the source basis and the research context are digitally accessible and documented well enough to support systematic examination.

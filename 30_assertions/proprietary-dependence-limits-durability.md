# Dependence on proprietary frontier systems introduces costs, reduces control, and sits in tension with durability

## Statement

The documented practice depends substantially on paid access to proprietary frontier models and capable agentic tools, and how far the method remains effective with local, open-weight, less capable, or more narrowly specialised systems is not established. Proprietary dependence introduces direct costs, reduces control over system changes, and sits in tension with the inspectability, reproducibility, and durability sought for research software and data, and model and harness changes may alter system behaviour even where project knowledge and research data remain stable.

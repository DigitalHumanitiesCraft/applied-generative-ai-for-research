# Social fluency, confidence and empathy in a generated answer are no evidence of correctness

## Statement

Models trained extensively on human communication can generate convincing social behaviour. Clear communication can improve an interaction, and the qualities that make it convincing carry no information about whether the claims inside it hold.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e59]] — states that convincing social behaviour is not evidence of a human interlocutor or of correct claims

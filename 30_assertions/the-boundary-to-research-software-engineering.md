# The boundary to Research Software Engineering is reached when an artefact takes on obligations the method does not carry

## Statement

Collaboration with Research Software Engineers remains essential for artefacts that require dependable long-term operation, institutional integration, security, or supported use by third parties, and the expertise and resources for that work are not equally available to individual researchers and small projects. Promptotyping is therefore not a substitute for such collaboration, because artefacts intended for dependable institutional or public operation continue to require expertise in security, accessibility, maintainability, integration, and sustained operation. The method is inappropriate where contributors cannot reconstruct or assess the relation among sources, research data, maintained project knowledge, implementation, and output.

# Critical expertise and agentic work may be distributed, and the distribution transfers no responsibility

## Statement

Critical expertise may be held by one hybrid scholar-developer or distributed among contributors with complementary scholarly and technical competencies, and agentic work may be divided among agents assigned to bounded components or checks. Assignments and permissions remain explicit and auditable, access to tools and project resources is limited to the delegated task, and the actions and outputs of agents remain inspectable against the project knowledge, sources, and criteria. Increasing the number of agents does not reduce the need for responsible judgement and may increase the work required to coordinate and audit their actions.

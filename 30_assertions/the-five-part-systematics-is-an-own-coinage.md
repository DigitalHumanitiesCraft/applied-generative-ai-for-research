# The five-part systematics of transformations is the author's own coinage derived over two axes

## Statement

No canonical taxonomy of document transformations exists in either contributing discipline, and an established classification of knowledge processes lies across the document operations rather than along them. The systematics is derived as a facetted classification over two axes, at the unit boundaries by dividing or merging and at the unit itself by reducing content, extending content or reordering form at constant content, and its completeness holds given that definition of the axes while composite procedures are compositions of the basic operations.

# A finding from an interface is attributed to the domain or to the representation before it is written back

## Statement

Some findings concerned the represented domain and required revision of the data model or capture practice. Others concerned how an otherwise adequate distinction should be made accessible and could remain within the operational or visual design of the interface. The attribution decides which document is revised, and it precedes the revision.

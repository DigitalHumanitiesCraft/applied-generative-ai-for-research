# What a model holds in its parameters carries no provenance for the individual claim

## Statement

Training changes parameters so that statistical structure from the training data influences later generation, and no addressable copy of a training document remains. A model can therefore describe a matter accurately while being unable to name the source from which the description was learned, and it holds no reliable register of pages or sources.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-de#^s17]] — states that pretraining representations are not addressable like database entries and that no source register exists
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e25]] — states that no addressable copy of a training document is retrieved
- [[20_distillates/documents/lecture-notes-en#^e26]] — states that accurate description and provenance for the claim come apart

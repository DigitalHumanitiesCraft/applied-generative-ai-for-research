# A research workflow combines probabilistic interpretation with deterministic checking rather than choosing between them

## Statement

Recognising a table, reading a layout or extracting values from noisy material may require probabilistic behaviour, and once values are represented explicitly the relations among them can be recomputed deterministically. The recurring pattern runs from probabilistic interpretation through structured representation to deterministic checking, and invariants such as row and column totals expose internal inconsistency without proving that the source was read correctly.

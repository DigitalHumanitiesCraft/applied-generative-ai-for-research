# A digital research artefact is a project-specific operational form through which researchers work with structured research data for a defined scholarly purpose

## Statement

What counts as research data depends on the disciplinary and project context in which material is selected and interpreted, and structured research data are selective representations in which particular aspects of that material are made computationally explicit. A digital research artefact is the operational form in which such data are worked with for a defined scholarly purpose, which distinguishes it from the data by its function within the research process rather than by its medium.

# A transformation may conform to its mapping and still represent the source inadequately

## Statement

A transformation may conform to its mapping and produce schema-valid output while representing the source inadequately, and an error in the implementation of an adequate mapping is corrected in the transformation rather than in the mapping. The implemented result therefore allows conformity to the specified mapping to be examined separately from the scholarly adequacy of that mapping.

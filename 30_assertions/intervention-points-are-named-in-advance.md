# The points at which a person has to intervene are named in advance

## Statement

They are contradictory requirements, missing scholarly foundations, changes that are hard to reverse, sensitive resources, modelling decisions with consequences in the matter, and validation and acceptance. Naming them before a run turns intervention into part of the design rather than a reaction to a problem.

# Research data are selected and constructed representations, so different representations of one object are not interchangeable

## Statement

Data are representations of objects, sources, observations or processes that scholarly work selects, collects, constructs or produces, and they need documentation, context and preservation to carry a claim. A facsimile, a transcription and an encoded representation of the same document therefore have different affordances and different forms of uncertainty.

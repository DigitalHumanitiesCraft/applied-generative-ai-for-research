# The formalisation target of Knowledge Engineering has shifted from logic and ontology to structured natural language

## Statement

The term comes from the expert-system tradition, and what has changed is what the knowledge is formalised into. Structured natural language with a light metadata component now suffices, because the language model supplies the language understanding that a formal representation previously had to encode.

# Five transformations convert raw material into reusable knowledge units

## Statement

Knowledge transformations are systematic operations that turn information into reusable, contextualised structures, and the five that carry the procedure are compression or distillation, normalisation, enrichment, consolidation and atomisation. Each is defined by its input, its output and the direction of its information flow.

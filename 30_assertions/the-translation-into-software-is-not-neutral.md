# The arrangement automates no neutral translation of research data into software

## Statement

What the layered arrangement does is make explicit the part of the translation that can be formulated, documented and checked. Responsibility for the interpretation of the data, for the scholarly adequacy of the modelling and for the acceptance of an artefact stays with the persons responsible for the research.

- [[20_distillates/documents/slide-deck#^k6]] — denies the neutral translation and names what the arrangement makes explicit instead

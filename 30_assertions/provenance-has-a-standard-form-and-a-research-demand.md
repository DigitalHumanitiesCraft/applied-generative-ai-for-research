# Provenance carries a machine-readable standard and a research-practical demand behind it

## Statement

Its machine-readable form is the W3C provenance recommendation with entity, activity, agent and a derivation relation, and its research-practical demand stands in the FAIR principles. Putting knowledge into filable and retrievable documents is knowledge codification with a stage model of capturing, packaging and reusing, and raising implicit expert knowledge corresponds to the externalisation mode of an organisational knowledge creation cycle, which makes the assignment to operations on single documents a transfer.

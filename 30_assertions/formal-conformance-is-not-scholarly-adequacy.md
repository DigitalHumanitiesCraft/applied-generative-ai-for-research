# Formal conformance establishes that rules were observed and never that a representation is adequate

## Statement

Valid syntax proves that the syntax is right and schema conformance proves that the formal rules were kept. Whether the transcription corresponds to the source, whether editorial uncertainty is represented adequately, whether an interface supports the intended interpretative actions and whether modelling decisions are made visible or naturalised are questions of a different kind, and an artefact can pass every formal check while failing all of them.

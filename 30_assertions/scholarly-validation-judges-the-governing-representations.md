# Scholarly validation judges whether the representations, requirements, and criteria governing a workflow are warranted and adequate

## Statement

Scholarly validation assesses whether the representations, requirements, evaluation criteria, and artefacts governing a workflow are warranted by the research material and adequate for their intended scholarly purpose. Its object is therefore the governing arrangement rather than a single output.

# The persistent project knowledge base and an agent's task-specific working context are distinct, and selection belongs to Context Engineering

## Statement

The knowledge base preserves the project's maintained understanding and decisions, while the working context holds the information and access required for a particular assignment and may draw on retrieval and direct access to project resources. Those resources supplement rather than replace the maintained account of purpose, data semantics, and criteria of assessment. Promptotyping is not tied to a particular documentary environment, and it treats selection as part of Context Engineering rather than using accumulation as a substitute for it.

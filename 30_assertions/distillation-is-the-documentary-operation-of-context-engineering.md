# Distillation is the principal documentary operation of Context Engineering within Promptotyping

## Statement

Distillation translates the understanding produced through Preparation and Exploration into the maintained knowledge documents from which implementation and verification proceed. It represents the project knowledge required for the current purpose while leaving the fuller sources and research data available for direct inspection and computational processing.

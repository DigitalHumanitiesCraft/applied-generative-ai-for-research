# Findings in interface and visual representation concern the claims implied by the way data are made visible and actionable

## Statement

Where dates encoded with an earliest and a latest bound were shown as precise points on a timeline, the representation claimed a precision the records did not carry, and representing them as intervals preserved the uncertainty already encoded in the structured data. The resulting write-back concerned the visual and operational treatment of uncertainty rather than the underlying records.

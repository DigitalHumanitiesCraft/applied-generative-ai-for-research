# Extended agentic work imposes explicit organisational requirements before it starts

## Statement

Tasks must be bounded and decomposed, tools, permissions and stopping conditions defined, intermediate results inspected and where necessary escalated, and project state kept understandable across steps. Agentic capability is therefore a new engineering surface on which context, state, tools, control and evaluation are coordinated over time rather than an increase in autonomy.

# The epistemic relevance of an artefact arises through operationalisation, examination, interpretation, attribution, and write-back

## Statement

An artefact does not produce scholarly knowledge autonomously. Maintained project knowledge gives provisional form to a project's understanding, implementation translates part of it into a functional artefact, and contributors then examine the consequences, attribute observed problems to the layer that produced them, and incorporate what matters into revised project knowledge. Generation and functionality alone establish nothing.

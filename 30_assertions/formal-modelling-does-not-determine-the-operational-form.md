# Formal modelling and FAIR preparation support interoperability without determining how researchers work with the data

## Statement

Modelling makes selected aspects of a domain explicit and relates them to a scholarly purpose, and formalisation through project-specific models, community standards, or Semantic Web technologies supports machine-actionability, interoperability, and reuse. The same data may support different research questions and therefore require different operational forms. Two contrasting use cases on a shared model of historical accounting transactions showed exactly this, in that the interoperable representation supported both investigations while determining neither which distinctions should be foregrounded nor which operations and interface structures each investigation required.

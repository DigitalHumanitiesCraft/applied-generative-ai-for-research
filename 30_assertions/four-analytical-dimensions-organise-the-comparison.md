# The comparison of promptotypes is organised by project condition, methodological function, operational form, and level of finding

## Statement

The four dimensions are the project condition from which implementation began, the primary methodological function implementation performed, the operational form of the resulting artefact, and the level at which examination produced a consequential finding. They may overlap and change between iterations, so a data-capture interface may also support exploration, an edition environment may combine transformation, editorial examination, and verification, and a production workflow may later become a handover package or itself an object of methodological examination.

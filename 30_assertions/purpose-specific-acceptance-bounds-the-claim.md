# Accepting a demonstrator for a proposal establishes no corpus-wide adequacy and licenses no transfer into production

## Statement

The acceptance of the proposal-stage artefact did not establish corpus-wide philological adequacy and did not imply that the provisional transformation, model, or interface could be transferred unchanged into later production. Acceptance can therefore stabilise a deliberately bounded project state for communication and decision-making without extending its claims beyond the materials examined and the purpose for which it was developed.

## Open questions

- The epistemic typology of interfaces in the outline names five types, while the paper names six operational forms of a promptotype. Which of the two the book carries, and how the audit interface is grounded, is open.
- Which of the project cases are documented well enough in the sources to carry a comparison, and which need material that is not yet in the vault?
- The critique of tool positivism that the outline announces for the artefact chapter has no term of its own in the sources, so it rests on the assertion that software participates in knowledge production plus the book's own conclusion.
- The teaching material of Part V is fed by the lecture notes, which belong to the other writing lane and are not yet distilled here.

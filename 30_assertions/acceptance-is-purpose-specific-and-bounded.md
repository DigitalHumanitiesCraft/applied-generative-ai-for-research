# Acceptance is the accountable decision that makes an iteration a promptotype for a stated purpose, and it is bounded by that purpose

## Statement

Acceptance is distinct from every form of checking that prepares it. It may be bounded, so that an artefact is accepted as an experimental processing pipeline or a handover state without being accepted as a completed or publication-ready scholarly edition. It does not imply that the project knowledge, the research data, or the artefact has become final, and a promptotype may be accepted as an exploratory interface, an internal production workflow, a proposal-stage demonstrator, a handover state, or a public research artefact, while new sources, corrected assumptions, changed purposes, or improved checks may initiate another iteration.

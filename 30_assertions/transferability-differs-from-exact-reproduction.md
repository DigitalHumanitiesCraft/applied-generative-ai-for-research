# Successful transfer requires the methodological relations to be re-established rather than identical outputs to be reproduced

## Statement

Another practitioner may apply the method while producing a different artefact with different documents, models, tools, and project structures. What transfer requires is that the core methodological relations be established, applied, and reconstructed without undocumented intervention by the originating scholar-developer.

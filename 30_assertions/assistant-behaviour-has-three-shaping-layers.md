# Training artefacts, character training and runtime instructions shape assistant behaviour at three different points

## Statement

A published specification or constitution can shape what is learned during development, character training and posttraining can stabilise behavioural dispositions, and system prompts operate at runtime inside a deployment context. The behaviour a user meets is produced by the interaction of trained parameters with the current runtime context, so the three must not be collapsed into one explanation.

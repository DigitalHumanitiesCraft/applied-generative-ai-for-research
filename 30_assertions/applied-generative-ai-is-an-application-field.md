# Applied generative AI is the adaptation of generative methods to domain problems rather than the development of models

## Statement

The field applies and adapts generative methods and systems to domain-specific problems in the tradition of applied computer science. Its object is the integration of these systems into existing knowledge practices and the methodological consequences of that integration.
